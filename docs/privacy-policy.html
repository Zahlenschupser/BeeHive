Privacy Policy for BeeHive

Status: August 29, 2026

Thank you for playing the “BeeHive” app.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information in connection with your use of the App.

1. Data Controller

Marvin Wenzel
humanoid0815@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

We strive to collect as little personal data as possible directly from you. The App is designed to function without creating a user account or directly entering personally identifiable information such as name, address, or phone number.

Automatically Collected Information (via Third-Party SDKs)

When you use the App and advertisements are displayed, our third-party advertising partner Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information. This includes:

  • IP Address: For approximate location determination and to deliver relevant advertising.
  • Advertising ID (e.g. Google Advertising ID — GAID): A unique, user-resettable ID for advertising purposes on mobile devices.
  • Device Information: Type of mobile device, operating system version, device settings (e.g. language).
  • App Usage Information: Information about how you interact with advertising in the App.
  • Consent Information: Via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), we collect your consent decisions regarding personalized advertising where required by law.

We do not operate our own analytics SDK or crash reporting service in the current version.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

BeeHive stores gameplay data only on your device, including colony save state (resources, buildings, bees, research, upgrades, and similar progress), language preference, app-wide high score, and optional player notes or progress log entries if you use those features. This data is not uploaded to our servers.

Information You Provide Directly to Us

We do not collect personal data directly from you through input fields in the App, unless you contact us by email outside the App. In that case, we process your email address and the information contained in your request to respond to your inquiry.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and Improving the App: To provide the core functions of the App (colony simulation, local save).
  • Displaying Advertising: To display advertising via Google AdMob, which enables the free provision of the App. This may include non-personalized and personalized advertising, provided you have given your explicit consent where required.
  • Measuring Ad Performance: To analyze the effectiveness of advertising campaigns (handled by Google).
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations: To comply with our legal obligations, particularly regarding the collection and management of advertising consents.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To protect our services and prevent fraudulent activities.
  • Communication with You: To respond to your inquiries when you contact us by email.

4. Sharing Your Information with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share or allow access to information as follows:

  • Advertising Partners (Google AdMob): We use Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC (or affiliated companies), to display advertising in our App. Google AdMob may collect and process information to display personalized or non-personalized advertising. We recommend that you read Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if we are legally required to do so or if we believe in good faith that such action is necessary.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Data Storage and Retention

The BeeHive app is designed to store all gameplay and progress data locally on your device only. We do not collect or store colony save data on any external servers operated by us. This data remains on your device for as long as you use the App or until you choose to delete it.

Information automatically collected by third-party SDKs (like Google AdMob) is processed and stored by those third parties according to their respective privacy policies.

Information you send to us by email will be retained for as long as necessary to process your request and for our records.

Data Deletion

You have control over the data stored locally by BeeHive on your device. You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • New colony: Starting a new colony (with confirmation in the App) overwrites your previous save on the device.
  • Clear app data: Android Settings → Apps → BeeHive → Storage → Clear data.
  • App uninstallation: Uninstalling BeeHive from your device permanently deletes all locally stored application data from that device.
  • Ad privacy choices: Where available, use Legal honeycomb → Ad privacy settings in the App, or your device’s Advertising ID settings, to limit personalized ads.

If you have questions about data deletion not covered above, contact us at humanoid0815@gmail.com.

6. Your Data Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws (including GDPR in the EEA/UK and similar laws elsewhere), you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. These may include:

  • Right to Information: The right to receive information about the data we process about you.
  • Right to Object: The right to object to certain data processing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent (e.g. personalized ads), you may withdraw consent via UMP or device settings.

You can restrict the use of your Advertising ID for personalized advertising in your mobile device settings or reset the ID.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or a higher age, depending on the laws of your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under this age.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy at this URL and updating the effective date at the top. A summary is also available in the App under Legal honeycomb.

9. Contact
